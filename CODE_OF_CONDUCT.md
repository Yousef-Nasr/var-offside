# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and respectful, including when you disagree
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

Football rivalries stay out of it. Argue about the code and the calls, not the clubs or the people.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/Yousef-Nasr/var-offside/security/advisories/new) or by contacting a maintainer directly on GitHub. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers follow the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, depending on the impact.
